如何理解向量 v=(ab)∈R2\mathbf v=\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}\in\mathbb R^2v=(ab​)∈R2？

课堂速览 · 看提示，不必照读整页

SAY · 你说

Hi everyone, I'm Xiaoxiao. Which lecture group are you in, and how far have you got? A plane vector has horizontal and vertical components. The complex number a plus b i corresponds to the point with coordinates a and b. We add matching components; real scaling changes both.
先确认讲座分流和实际进度；回顾平面向量两个分量、复数与平面点的对应、按分量相加和数乘。

CHECK · 具体检查

Where would you plot three minus two i? What is its imaginary part?
坐标 (3,−2)，右下方；虚部 −2。若混淆，先画带正方向的两条轴。
SAY · 你说
Hi everyone. I'm Xiaoxiao, your Linear Algebra tutor. We will work on questions together. Please ask questions, and be ready to explain a step rather than just give a number.

SAY · 你说
Which lecture group are you in, and how far have you got? Have you covered polar coordinates and the dot product yet?
你们属于哪个讲座分流班？实际讲到哪里了？极坐标和点积已经学过了吗？
动作 · 不读出

BOARD · 板书
(ab)∈R2⟷a+bi∈C,i2=−1\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}\in\mathbb R^2\qquad\longleftrightarrow\qquad a+bi\in\mathbb C,\quad i^2=-1(ab​)∈R2⟷a+bi∈C,i2=−1
SAY · 你说
A vector in the plane has two components. The first is horizontal, and the second is vertical. We can draw it as an arrow from the origin. A complex number a plus b i corresponds to the same point: a on the real axis, and b on the imaginary axis.
平面向量有两个分量：第一个水平，第二个竖直，可画成从原点出发的箭头。复数 a+bi 对应同一点：实轴坐标 a，虚轴坐标 b。
教师备注
这里建立平面上的对应，不说复数乘法就是向量逐分量相乘。虚部是实数 b，不是 bi。向量可平移画出，但本节用从原点出发的表示最方便。
BOARD · 板书
(ab)+(cd)=(a+cb+d),t(ab)=(tatb)\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}c\\d\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}a+c\\b+d\end{pmatrix},\qquad t\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}ta\\tb\end{pmatrix}(ab​)+(cd​)=(a+cb+d​),t(ab​)=(tatb​)
SAY · 你说
We add matching components. Multiplying by a real scalar multiplies both components. We can describe a nonzero arrow in another way too: by its length and its direction. That will connect our coordinate questions.
向量相加对应分量分别相加；乘实数时两个分量都乘。非零箭头也能由长度和方向描述，这就连接到后面的坐标题。
SAY · 你说
For three minus two i, what are the horizontal and vertical coordinates? Where would the point sit?
对于 3−2i，水平和竖直坐标各是多少？点在哪里？
动作 · 不读出

ANSWER · 先听学生，再核对
The coordinates are three and minus two, so it is to the right and below the origin. Its imaginary part is minus two, not minus two i.
坐标是 (3,−2)，在原点右下方。虚部是 −2，不是 −2i。

展开完整题目（不含答案）
How many different ways can we represent the complex number 2−i2-i2−i?

SAY · 你说

Sketch two minus i. Compare with your partner: what are its coordinates, its length, and its direction? We are not finding different complex numbers. We are describing the same number in different ways. Use the picture before choosing an angle.

DO · 接下来做什么

CHECK · 具体检查

Why is a positive acute angle wrong for this point?

SAY · 你说
How many different ways can we represent the complex number two minus i? First sketch it on an Argand diagram. Then compare with the person next to you. Can you give a vector representation, and perhaps a polar representation?

z=2−iz=2-iz=2−i
动作 · 不读出

SAY · 你说

ANSWER · 先听学生，再核对
It is in the fourth quadrant, at two, minus one. Its distance from the origin is the square root of five. The picture tells us the angle is below the positive real axis.
位于第四象限 (2,−1)，模为 √5。图说明方向在正实轴下方。
BOARD · 板书
2−i ⟷ (2−1),r=5,θ=−arctan⁡(1/2)(mod2π)2-i\ \longleftrightarrow\ \begin{pmatrix}2\\-1\end{pmatrix},\quad r=\sqrt5,\quad \theta=-\arctan(1/2)\pmod{2\pi}2−i ⟷ (2−1​),r=5​,θ=−arctan(1/2)(mod2π)
BOARD · 板书

SAY · 你说
These are different descriptions of the same point. An angle can have more than one numerical representative, so always check the requested interval.
这些是同一点的不同描述。角度可有多个数值代表，因此要检查题目要求的范围。

展开完整题目（不含答案）
Let u=(40)\mathbf u=\begin{pmatrix}4\\0\end{pmatrix}u=(40​), v=(12)\mathbf v=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix}v=(12​), w=(1−2)\mathbf w=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-2\end{pmatrix}w=(1−2​). 设 u=(40)\mathbf u=\begin{pmatrix}4\\0\end{pmatrix}u=(40​)、v=(12)\mathbf v=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix}v=(12​)、w=(1−2)\mathbf w=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-2\end{pmatrix}w=(1−2​)。求 x,y∈R2\mathbf x,\mathbf y\in\mathbb R^2x,y∈R2，使 x=3u−2(v+w)\mathbf x=3\mathbf u-2(\mathbf v+\mathbf w)x=3u−2(v+w) 且 w−y=y−u\mathbf w-\mathbf y=\mathbf y-\mathbf uw−y=y−u。

课堂速览 · 看提示，不必照读整页

SAY · 你说

Find x and y in pairs. Start with the bracket for x. For y, collect the two copies of y before dividing by two. Be ready to explain one step. When you finish, check y in the original equation, not just in your rearranged version.

CHECK · 具体检查

What are w minus y and y minus u when y is five halves, minus one?

SAY · 你说

BOARD · 板书
u=(40),v=(12),w=(1−2),x,y∈R2u=\begin{pmatrix}4\\0\end{pmatrix},\quad v=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix},\quad w=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-2\end{pmatrix},\quad x,y\in\mathbb R^2u=(40​),v=(12​),w=(1−2​),x,y∈R2
BOARD · 板书

动作 · 不读出
巡视看两点：−2 是否分配到整个 v+w；第二式是否真的把两份 y 合并。先问卡在哪一步，不立刻把答案写出。三分钟后请两组各解释一个式子。
SAY · 你说
For x, which bracket can you simplify first? For y, can you collect both copies of y on the same side?

BOARD · 板书
v+w=(20),x=(120)−(40)=(80)v+w=\begin{pmatrix}2\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad x=\begin{pmatrix}12\\0\end{pmatrix}-\begin{pmatrix}4\\0\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}8\\0\end{pmatrix}v+w=(20​),x=(120​)−(40​)=(80​)
ANSWER · 先听学生，再核对
The vertical components of v and w cancel. That gives x equal to eight, zero. From w minus y equals y minus u, adding y and u gives u plus w equals two y.
v 和 w 的竖直分量抵消，所以 x=(8,0)。第二式两边加 y 和 u，得到 u+w=2y。
BOARD · 板书
2y=u+w=(5−2),y=(5/2−1)2y=u+w=\begin{pmatrix}5\\-2\end{pmatrix},\qquad y=\begin{pmatrix}5/2\\-1\end{pmatrix}2y=u+w=(5−2​),y=(5/2−1​)
SAY · 你说
How can we check y without repeating exactly the same rearrangement? Substitute it into both sides of the original equation.
不用重复同一套移项，怎样检查 y？把它代回原方程左右两边。
BOARD · 板书
w−y=(−3/2−1)=y−uw-y=\begin{pmatrix}-3/2\\-1\end{pmatrix}=y-uw−y=(−3/2−1​)=y−u
ANSWER · 先听学生，再核对
Geometrically, y is the midpoint of the position vectors u and w. Both sides describe the same displacement from u to y and from y to w.
从图看，y 是 u、w 对应点的中点；从 u 到 y 与从 y 到 w 的位移相同。
BACKUP · 按需使用

提前完成就解释为什么是中点，或画 u、w、y。比再报一个未经检查的答案更重要的是解释。
