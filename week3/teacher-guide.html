Pure Mathematics

Boardwork · Pure Mathematics

Start with the definition

把整除写成等式

  1. For integers m and n, m divides n when n is an integer multiple of m. The witness k must be an integer.

    m∣n  ⟺  ∃k∈Z: n=mkm\mid n\iff\exists k\in\mathbb Z:\ n=mkm∣n⟺∃k∈Z: n=mk
    m∣n  ⟺  ∃k∈Zsuch that n=mk\begin{gathered}m\mid n\iff\exists k\in\mathbb Z\\\text{such that }n=mk\end{gathered}m∣n⟺∃k∈Zsuch that n=mk​

    板书先写域；强调不是任意实数倍。

  2. For example, 4 divides 12 because 12=4·3, with 3 an integer. But 4 does not divide 6: the only possible witness is 3/2, which is not an integer.

    12=4⋅3,6=4⋅3212=4\cdot3,\qquad 6=4\cdot\frac3212=4⋅3,6=4⋅23​
    12=4⋅36=4⋅32\begin{gathered}12=4\cdot3\\6=4\cdot\frac32\end{gathered}12=4⋅36=4⋅23​​

    用同一形式对比真与假。

  3. Similarly, to prove an integer n is even, write n=2k and explain why k is an integer.

    n is even  ⟺  ∃k∈Z: n=2kn\text{ is even}\iff\exists k\in\mathbb Z:\ n=2kn is even⟺∃k∈Z: n=2k
    n is even  ⟺  ∃k∈Z: n=2k\begin{gathered}n\text{ is even}\\\iff\exists k\in\mathbb Z:\ n=2k\end{gathered}n is even⟺∃k∈Z: n=2k​

    偶数也是整除定义；最后一句要交代整数性。

Check understanding · 检查是不是真懂了

Does 0 divide 0? Does 0 divide 5? Explain using the definition.

0 能整除 0 吗？能整除 5 吗？必须用定义解释。

Expected answer · 应听到的回答

Yes: 0=0·0 supplies an integer witness. No: 0·k=0 for every integer k, so it cannot equal 5.

前者能，取 k=0；后者不能，因为 0 乘任何整数都不是 5。

留意：m|n 应写成 n=mk，不要把方向反过来。

Boardwork · Pure Mathematics

When well-ordering applies

良序原理：先检查集合

  1. Well-ordering: every nonempty subset of the nonnegative integers has a least element. Both hypotheses matter.

    ∅≠S⊆Z≥0 ⟹ ∃s0∈S ∀s∈S: s0≤s\varnothing\ne S\subseteq\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists s_0\in S\ \forall s\in S:\ s_0\le s∅=S⊆Z≥0​ ⟹ ∃s0​∈S ∀s∈S: s0​≤s
    ∅≠S⊆Z≥0⟹∃s0∈S∀s∈S: s0≤s\begin{gathered}\varnothing\ne S\subseteq\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\\\Longrightarrow\exists s_0\in S\\\forall s\in S:\ s_0\le s\end{gathered}∅=S⊆Z≥0​⟹∃s0​∈S∀s∈S: s0​≤s​

    这里统一用非负整数，避免不同自然数记号约定。

  2. Take S to be the nonnegative integers strictly larger than 17/4. It contains 5,6,7,8,…, so it is nonempty. Since 4<17/4<5, every member is at least 5 and 5 itself is a member.

    S={m∈Z≥0:m>17/4},min⁡S=5S=\{m\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}:m>17/4\},\qquad\min S=5S={m∈Z≥0​:m>17/4},minS=5
    S={m∈Z≥0:m>17/4}min⁡S=5\begin{gathered}S=\{m\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}:m>17/4\}\\\min S=5\end{gathered}S={m∈Z≥0​:m>17/4}minS=5​

    存在性来自良序；本例最小值还要验证“属于集合＋不大于其他元素”。

  3. For the real-number set T, any proposed minimum t can be beaten by its midpoint with 17/4. Thus T has no least element.

    T={x∈R:x>17/4},u=t+17/42,17/4<u<tT=\{x\in\mathbb R:x>17/4\},\quad u=\frac{t+17/4}{2},\quad17/4<u<tT={x∈R:x>17/4},u=2t+17/4​,17/4<u<t
    T={x∈R:x>17/4}u=t+17/4217/4<u<t\begin{gathered}T=\{x\in\mathbb R:x>17/4\}\\u=\frac{t+17/4}{2}\\17/4<u<t\end{gathered}T={x∈R:x>17/4}u=2t+17/4​17/4<u<t​

    把中点写出来：仍在集合内，而且更小。

  4. The infimum of T is 17/4, but it is not in T. Also, the integers Z have no least element: for every n, n−1 is smaller.

    下确界不等于最小元；非空也不能单独推出最小元。

Check understanding · 检查是不是真懂了

Boardwork · Pure Mathematics

Quotient and remainder

商与余数：等式和区间都要对

  1. For integers b and a with a≠0, there are unique integers q,r with b=aq+r and 0≤r<|a|.

    b=aq+r,0≤r<∣a∣,q,r∈Zb=aq+r,\qquad0\le r<|a|,\qquad q,r\in\mathbb Zb=aq+r,0≤r<∣a∣,q,r∈Z
    b=aq+r,q,r∈Z0≤r<∣a∣\begin{gathered}b=aq+r,\qquad q,r\in\mathbb Z\\0\le r<|a|\end{gathered}b=aq+r,q,r∈Z0≤r<∣a∣​

    固定记号：b 被除，a 除数；负除数也用绝对值。

  2. For b=−29 and a=6, use the multiple −30, immediately below −29. The difference is 1.

    −29=6(−5)+1,q=−5, r=1-29=6(-5)+1,\qquad q=-5,\ r=1−29=6(−5)+1,q=−5, r=1
    −29=6(−5)+1q=−5,r=1\begin{gathered}-29=6(-5)+1\\q=-5,\quad r=1\end{gathered}−29=6(−5)+1q=−5,r=1​

    从较小的倍数往上补，余数才非负。

  3. The equality −29=6(−4)−5 is true, but its remainder −5 is outside [0,6). It is not the required division-algorithm representation.

    −29=6(−4)+(−5),−5<0-29=6(-4)+(-5),\qquad-5<0−29=6(−4)+(−5),−5<0
    −29=6(−4)+(−5)−5<0\begin{gathered}-29=6(-4)+(-5)\\-5<0\end{gathered}−29=6(−4)+(−5)−5<0​

    等式正确不代表满足定理全部条件。

  4. A negative divisor is allowed. With b=29 and a=−6, the required pair is q=−4,r=5.

    29=(−6)(−4)+5,0≤5<629=(-6)(-4)+5,\qquad0\le5<629=(−6)(−4)+5,0≤5<6
    29=(−6)(−4)+50≤5<6\begin{gathered}29=(-6)(-4)+5\\0\le5<6\end{gathered}29=(−6)(−4)+50≤5<6​

    单独代回等式，再单独检查区间。

  5. Without the remainder bounds, replacing (q,r) by (q+1,r−a) leaves aq+r unchanged; uniqueness would fail.

    a(q+1)+(r−a)=aq+ra(q+1)+(r-a)=aq+ra(q+1)+(r−a)=aq+r

    余数区间不是装饰，它负责排除其他表示。

Boardwork · Pure Mathematics

Why the pair is unique

为什么商和余数唯一

  1. Suppose the same integer has two valid representations, with both remainders in [0,6). We will show that the two pairs coincide.

    −29=6q+r=6q′+r′,0≤r,r′<6-29=6q+r=6q^{\prime}+r^{\prime},\qquad0\le r,r^{\prime}<6−29=6q+r=6q′+r′,0≤r,r′<6
    −29=6q+r=6q′+r′0≤r,r′<6\begin{gathered}-29=6q+r=6q^{\prime}+r^{\prime}\\0\le r,r^{\prime}<6\end{gathered}−29=6q+r=6q′+r′0≤r,r′<6​

    先假设两组都满足条件，不要只重复计算一组。

  2. Subtract the equalities. The difference between the remainders is a multiple of 6.

    6(q−q′)=r′−r6(q-q^{\prime})=r^{\prime}-r6(q−q′)=r′−r

    等式给出“是 6 的整数倍”。

  3. The remainder bounds force that multiple to lie strictly between −6 and 6. The only integer multiple of 6 in this interval is 0.

    −6<r′−r<6⟹r′−r=0-6<r^{\prime}-r<6\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r^{\prime}-r=0−6<r′−r<6⟹r′−r=0
    −6<r′−r<6⟹r′−r=0\begin{gathered}-6<r^{\prime}-r<6\\\Longrightarrow r^{\prime}-r=0\end{gathered}−6<r′−r<6⟹r′−r=0​

    区间给出“大小受限”；两个条件合起来才能判 0。

  4. Hence r′=r. Substitute back: 6(q−q′)=0, so q=q′. Both the quotient and remainder are unique.

    r′=r,q=q′r^{\prime}=r,\qquad q=q^{\prime}r′=r,q=q′

    先得余数相等，再用除数非零得到商相等。

  5. The same proof works for any nonzero divisor a: the remainder difference is a multiple of a of absolute value smaller than |a|, and is therefore zero.

    a(q−q′)=r′−r,∣r′−r∣<∣a∣a(q-q^{\prime})=r^{\prime}-r,\qquad|r^{\prime}-r|<|a|a(q−q′)=r′−r,∣r′−r∣<∣a∣
    a(q−q′)=r′−r∣r′−r∣<∣a∣\begin{gathered}a(q-q^{\prime})=r^{\prime}-r\\|r^{\prime}-r|<|a|\end{gathered}a(q−q′)=r′−r∣r′−r∣<∣a∣​

    从具体 6 提炼一般证明，但不省掉整数倍这个理由。

Prove it with integer witnesses

整除证明与反例

  1. Suppose 4|u and 4|v. By definition there are integers s,t with u=4s and v=4t.

    u=4s,v=4t,s,t∈Zu=4s,\quad v=4t,\qquad s,t\in\mathbb Zu=4s,v=4t,s,t∈Z
    u=4s,v=4ts,t∈Z\begin{gathered}u=4s,\qquad v=4t\\s,t\in\mathbb Z\end{gathered}u=4s,v=4ts,t∈Z​

    把两个前提分别展开，不用同一个未知数代替两个见证。

  2. Then 3u−2v=4(3s−2t). Since 3s−2t is an integer, this proves 4|(3u−2v).

    3u−2v=3(4s)−2(4t)=4(3s−2t)3u-2v=3(4s)-2(4t)=4(3s-2t)3u−2v=3(4s)−2(4t)=4(3s−2t)
    3u−2v=3(4s)−2(4t)=4(3s−2t)\begin{aligned}3u-2v&=3(4s)-2(4t)\\&=4(3s-2t)\end{aligned}3u−2v​=3(4s)−2(4t)=4(3s−2t)​

    最后指出括号里的整数，证明才闭合。

  3. The stronger conclusion 8|(3u−2v) does not follow. Set u=v=4: both hypotheses hold, but 3u−2v=4, which is not divisible by 8.

    u=v=4:4∣u, 4∣v,8∤(3u−2v)u=v=4:\quad4\mid u,\ 4\mid v,\quad8\nmid(3u-2v)u=v=4:4∣u, 4∣v,8∤(3u−2v)
    u=v=44∣u,4∣v8∤(3u−2v)\begin{gathered}u=v=4\\4\mid u,\qquad4\mid v\\8\nmid(3u-2v)\end{gathered}u=v=44∣u,4∣v8∤(3u−2v)​

    反例先核查前提，再指出结论失败。

  4. More generally, if d|u and d|v, then d divides every integer linear combination αu+βv.

    αu+βv=d(αs+βt),α,β,s,t∈Z\alpha u+\beta v=d(\alpha s+\beta t),\qquad\alpha,\beta,s,t\in\mathbb Zαu+βv=d(αs+βt),α,β,s,t∈Z
    αu+βv=d(αs+βt)α,β,s,t∈Z\begin{gathered}\alpha u+\beta v=d(\alpha s+\beta t)\\\alpha,\beta,s,t\in\mathbb Z\end{gathered}αu+βv=d(αs+βt)α,β,s,t∈Z​

    强调系数也须是整数。

Check understanding · 检查是不是真懂了

Expected answer · 应听到的回答

Write u=ds,v=dt; then u−v=d(s−t), with s−t an integer. Arbitrary real coefficients need not give an integer witness: 4|4 but 4 does not divide (1/2)·4=2.

留意：整除定义中的整数性不能省略；反例必须满足原来的全部前提。

Boardwork · Pure Mathematics

A certificate for the gcd

gcd 的两部分证书

  1. For 18 and 30, the positive common divisors are 1,2,3,6; their greatest is 6. We can certify this without relying only on a list.

    gcd⁡(18,30)=6\gcd(18,30)=6gcd(18,30)=6

    先交代 gcd 的含义，再给可推广的证明。

  2. First, 6 is a positive common divisor: 18=6·3 and 30=6·5.

    6>0,6∣18,6∣306>0,\qquad6\mid18,\qquad6\mid306>0,6∣18,6∣30

    第一部分：6 自己必须真的是公因子。

  3. Second, use the supplied identity 6=2·18−30. Let c be any positive common divisor, so 18=cs and 30=ct for integers s,t.

    6=2⋅18−30=2cs−ct=c(2s−t)6=2\cdot18-30=2cs-ct=c(2s-t)6=2⋅18−30=2cs−ct=c(2s−t)
    6=2⋅18−30=2cs−ct=c(2s−t)\begin{aligned}6&=2\cdot18-30\\&=2cs-ct\\&=c(2s-t)\end{aligned}6​=2⋅18−30=2cs−ct=c(2s−t)​

    直接使用给定系数，不预设扩展欧几里得算法。

  4. Thus c|6. Because c>0 and 6>0, 6=ck has k≥1, so c≤6. Therefore 6 is at least every positive common divisor and is itself one: it is the gcd.

    c∣6, c>0⟹c≤6c\mid6,\ c>0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad c\le6c∣6, c>0⟹c≤6
    c∣6,c>0⟹c≤6\begin{gathered}c\mid6,\qquad c>0\\\Longrightarrow c\le6\end{gathered}c∣6,c>0⟹c≤6​

    把“整除 6”到“不超过 6”的正性理由补完整。

  5. Every integer value of 18x+30y is divisible by 6. Hence 18x+30y=9 has no integer solution.

    18x+30y=6(3x+5y),6∤918x+30y=6(3x+5y),\qquad6\nmid918x+30y=6(3x+5y),6∤9
    18x+30y=6(3x+5y)6∤9\begin{gathered}18x+30y=6(3x+5y)\\6\nmid9\end{gathered}18x+30y=6(3x+5y)6∤9​

    这是证书的直接应用：先看整除条件，不盲目求解。

Check understanding · 检查是不是真懂了

Why does 48=18+30 not certify that the gcd is 48? State the two certificate conditions.

为什么 48 的线性组合等式不够？证书到底要哪两部分？

Expected answer · 应听到的回答

留意：任意线性组合并不自动等于 gcd。

    这张板书三个分支，每次回到原矩阵。
